# Tickle — context for Claude

Use this file as background when helping with Tickle's marketing: App Store listing, social posts,
landing page, ads, launch messages, emails, or support replies. Everything here is true as of
**2026-11-14**. If something isn't in this file, ask before claiming it.

---

## 1. What Tickle is

Tickle is an **iPhone planner and reminder app**. You add plans (a class, a shift, a deadline, rent,
the gym), and Tickle:

1. shows **what's next and how long until it starts**, in the app, in widgets, on the lock screen
   and as a live countdown;
2. **reminds you at the right moment**: up to 5 alerts per plan, or an alarm that rings;
3. shows **afterwards how well you kept up**, with streaks and weekly, monthly and yearly recaps.

It works fully in **Thai and English**. Thai is written the way people actually speak, not
machine-translated.

- **Name:** Tickle (the idea: a light tap on the shoulder, not nagging)
- **Platform:** iPhone (iOS). Android is planned but not started.
- **Stage:** in **TestFlight beta** with a small group of testers. **Not on the App Store yet.**
- **Maker:** an independent developer (solo, individual Apple Developer account)
- **Support email:** tickle.daily.app@gmail.com
- **Website (legal pages):** https://tickle-app.vercel.app (Privacy, Terms, Support)

## 2. Goal of the app

**User goal:** know what's next without thinking about it, get reminded without being nagged, and
see that you're keeping up.

**Product goal:** Tickle succeeds when people rely on its glanceable surfaces (widgets, lock
screen, Live Activity) to know what's next, and come back to their recaps to check their progress.

**Business goal:** a free tier that is genuinely useful, with an honest Premium upgrade for people
who plan a lot (more than 3 active plans) or want monthly and yearly recaps.

## 3. Who it's for (persona)

**Main persona: "Mint", 20–27, Bangkok**
- University student or in a first job. Juggles classes, deadlines, part-time shifts,
  appointments, bills and the gym.
- Thai is their first language and English their second. Uses an iPhone and has widgets on their
  home screen.
- Checks their phone many times a day **for a few seconds**, often one-handed between things.
  Wants to know "what's next and how long have I got?" at a glance.
- Has tried Apple Reminders, Notes or Google Calendar. Finds them either too bare (Reminders) or
  too heavy and work-like (Calendar), and none of them feel Thai.
- Dislikes apps that nag, guilt-trip, or lock basic features behind a paywall.
- Motivated by small wins: streaks, seeing a good week.

**Secondary:** English-speaking young adults anywhere who want a calm, good-looking planner with
great widgets and a live countdown.

**Usage pattern:** many short glances a day (mostly via widgets, lock screen and Live Activity);
less often, opening the app to add or edit a plan; weekly, a look at the recap.

## 4. How the app works

### Main screens (4 tabs)
- **Home (Today):** greeting, a week strip, and today's timeline of plans with a countdown to the
  next one. Missed plans are stated plainly ("missed", with undo), never scolded.
- **Calendar:** Week, Month and Year views. Coloured dots for plans, public holidays marked, swipe
  between months, tap a day to see or add plans.
- **Stats (Progress):** completion, streaks, trends and breakdown by colour, plus recaps.
- **Profile:** account, sync, notifications, appearance, language, theme colour, widgets guide,
  subscription.
- A floating **+** button adds a plan from anywhere.

### A plan can have
- title (with suggestions from past plans; you can remove ones you don't want)
- date and time: a single time, a time range, or all day, plus its own time zone
- **up to 5 alerts** (you pick how early), or an **Alarm** that rings with a chosen sound
  (6 built-in sounds) and has Stop and Snooze 5 min buttons
- **repeat:** daily, weekly, monthly or custom ("every 2 weeks on Mon and Thu"), with an end date
- colour, group (e.g. "Work", "Uni"), location (with address search), photo and notes
- a **Live** switch to show it as a live countdown

### Outside the app (a key selling point)
- **Live Activity and Dynamic Island:** a live countdown to the next plan on the lock screen.
- **Home-screen widgets (free, never expire):** Calendar (two-week or full month), Today, and
  Next 3 days.
- **Lock-screen widgets:** Up next, a to-do ring, and the date.
- **Notifications:** plan alerts, alarms, and an optional daily recap at a time you choose.

### Other details
- **Public holidays** on the calendar based on the phone's region (about 100+ countries). For
  Thailand this includes royal memorial days, shown respectfully.
- **Two languages:** Thai and English, switchable in the app.
- **Personalisation (free):** 8 theme colours plus a custom colour slider, light and dark mode,
  and adjustable text size.
- **Accounts:** sign in with email (Google sign-in exists) to sync across devices, or use
  **guest mode** with data kept only on the phone.
- **Age:** for users 13+.

## 5. Free vs Premium

| Free (forever) | Premium |
|---|---|
| 3 active plans | Unlimited plans |
| Today view and streak | Monthly and yearly recaps |
| Weekly recap | Everything in Free |
| All widgets, never expire | |

- **Price:** **฿89/month** or **฿555/year** in Thailand (about $2.69/month or $16.99/year in the
  US). The annual plan saves about 47%. Actual prices are set by the App Store per country.
- **7-day free trial.**
- **Billing:** handled entirely by Apple (charging, renewal, cancellation in iPhone Settings,
  refunds). Tickle reminds users 7, 3 and 1 days before Premium ends if auto-renew is off.
- **If Premium ends:** nothing is deleted. All plans stay; the user just can't add more than 3
  active ones until some finish or they renew.
- **Paywall headline (current):** "Plan without limits" / "วางแผนได้ไม่จำกัด". Sub-line: "Unlimited
  plans, plus monthly and yearly recaps."

## 6. Positioning: why Tickle

1. **Live countdowns:** see exactly how long until your next plan, even on the lock screen.
2. **Glanceable, free widgets:** the best parts aren't paywalled and don't expire.
3. **Truly Thai:** native Thai copy, Thai holidays (including royal memorial days), and Buddhist
   year display. Not a translated Western app.
4. **Recaps and streaks:** see how you're keeping up, which encourages without guilt.
5. **Calm, not naggy:** reminders when you ask for them, otherwise quiet. "I don't chase."

**One-line pitches (existing in-app copy you can reuse):**
- "Everything you owe today, in one list." / "ทุกอย่างที่ต้องทำวันนี้ รวมไว้ในที่เดียว"
- "Set it once, it keeps coming back." / "ตั้งครั้งเดียว วนกลับมาเตือนเรื่อยๆ"
- "Let me tap you on the shoulder." / "ให้เราช่วยสะกิดเตือนนะ"

## 7. Brand voice and rules (must follow)

- **Tone:** friendly, light and calm. A gentle tap on the shoulder. Short sentences. Tickle can
  speak in the first person ("Let me tap you on the shoulder").
- **Thai must sound native,** like a Thai person wrote it, never machine-translated or "AI". Prefer
  everyday words (e.g. เลยเวลา, ทำใหม่). Avoid leaving a single orphaned word on the last line.
- **Honest Premium:** only mention features that really exist and are really in Premium. No
  invented features, no superlatives ("the best", "#1"), no fake urgency.
- **Never invent proof:** there are **no reviews, ratings, user counts, testimonials or press
  yet.** Do not make any up, and don't imply them ("loved by thousands").
- **Holidays are respectful:** no celebratory emoji on holidays, especially royal memorial days.
- **Encourage, don't scold:** missed plans are stated plainly; never shame the user.
- **Visual identity:** the main colour is "Tickle blue" (azure). The app has a small round mascot
  (Tickle) with eyes and a speech bubble, used in the icon and splash. Premium visuals always use
  Tickle blue.
- **Font in the app:** Anuphan (supports Thai and Latin).

## 8. Facts to keep straight

- iPhone only for now. Don't promise Android or iPad dates.
- No calendar sync with Google or Apple Calendar (an idea, not built). Don't claim it.
- No sharing or collaboration between users.
- No AI features. Don't describe Tickle as AI-powered.
- Data: plans are stored on the phone, and also in the cloud (Supabase) when signed in. See the
  Privacy policy. No ads and no selling of data.
- Not on the App Store yet, so no App Store link. For beta testers, distribution is TestFlight.
