Routine

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Routine ("the app," "we," "our") is a personal planning and routine-tracking app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. If you're just browsing as a guest, most of this won't apply to you — see "Guest mode" below.

Information we collect

What's collected depends on how you use the app:

  • Guest mode — nothing leaves your device. Your plans, groups, and settings are stored locally only.
  • Account sign-in — if you create an account (email/password, or "Continue with Google" where available), we collect your email address and, for password sign-up, a securely hashed password. We do not see or store your password in plain text.
  • Profile — your display name and chosen avatar color.
  • Plan & routine data — the plans, groups, and schedules you create: titles, dates, times, locations, notes, colors, and completion status. This syncs to your account so it's available if you sign in on another device.
  • Photos — if you attach a photo to a plan, the photo itself stays on your device. Only a local file reference is saved with your account data, not the image, so photos currently don't carry over to a different device.

We don't use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and we don't collect any data beyond what's listed above.

How we use your information

Solely to operate the app for you: authenticating your account, storing and syncing your plans across your signed-in devices, and showing your data back to you. We don't sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — our backend provider, hosting authentication and your account data. Access to your data is restricted to your own account by row-level security.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google handles that authentication step directly; we only receive your email address to create your account.

Other sign-in options (such as Sign in with Apple) may be added in a future update — this policy will be updated to reflect that when it happens.

Data retention & deletion

You can erase your plans and groups at any time from Profile → Data Privacy → Clear All Data, which removes that data from both your device and your account.

To delete your account entirely (including your email and login), contact us at the address below and we'll process the deletion. In-app account deletion is planned for a future update.

Children's privacy

Routine is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
